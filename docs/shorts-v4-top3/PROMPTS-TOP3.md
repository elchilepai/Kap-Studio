*Prompts escritos para un generador externo con la referencia de KAP adjunta. No se ha generado ninguna imagen. Validación estructural sin modelo.*

## 1. Ficha global de estilo (reutilizable)

Professional 2D stickman-style cartoon illustration, clean and cinematic. Thick confident black outlines, flat cel colors with soft shading and subtle gradients, hand-inked texture strokes in the environment (wood grain, cracks, brick lines, fabric folds). Characters have big round white heads with simple black dot eyes. Richly detailed, believable backgrounds with three clear depth layers (foreground, midground, background) and slight atmospheric perspective. One strong, motivated light source per image (window light, screen glow, lamp, street light) with warm/cool contrast. Strong, varied composition and clear visual storytelling. Looks like a frame from a premium animated explainer channel, not a presentation slide.

**Formato:** Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio (1080x1920), single still image. Keep the top 12% and the bottom 20% free of important details (subtitle and app UI safe area).

**Evitar siempre:** photorealistic, 3D render, generic flat vector clipart, stock illustration, corporate presentation style, childish crayon drawing, empty plain background, extra characters, extra fingers, redesigned KAP, brand names or logos, watermark, random or gibberish text, blurry text, motion blur, speed lines, animation frames, comic panels.
